Requirements

Scaffolding Contractor
Panel Requirements

To join the National Scaffold contractor panel, your company must meet these requirements:

  • ✓

    Public Liability Insurance

    Minimum £2 million public liability insurance. We'll ask for a copy of your certificate during vetting.

  • ✓

    CISRS Trained Operatives

    Your scaffolders must hold valid CISRS cards. We're committed to safety and professionalism on every job.

  • ✓

    Registered Business

    You must be a registered UK business — sole trader, partnership or limited company. No cash-in-hand operations.

  • ✓

    Professional References

    We'll ask for 2-3 references from previous customers or contractors. We take our vetting seriously to protect our customers.

  • ✓

    Commitment to Fixed Pricing

    You must be able to work within our fixed price framework. Our promise to customers is no hidden charges — we need our panel to uphold this.

  • ✓

    Response Time

    Panel contractors are expected to respond to leads within 2 hours during business hours. Speed of response is critical to our customer promise.
