Types of Scaffolding Explained

📅 July 2026  ·  6 min read  ·  By National Scaffold

"Scaffolding" covers a wide range of very different jobs — a chimney repair needs a completely different access setup to a full house extension or a retail unit refit. Here's a plain-English guide to the main types we provide, so you know exactly what to ask for when you get your quote.

Residential Scaffolding

Scaffolding for homes, covering everything from a single chimney stack to a full elevation on a detached house. Most residential jobs fall into one of the categories below — but if your project doesn't fit neatly into one, our vetted contractors can quote for it directly.

🏠 Residential Scaffolding (general)

The umbrella category for any home scaffolding job — roofing, gutters, chimneys, extensions, render and more, all on one fixed price.

See residential scaffolding →

🧱 Chimney Scaffolding

Safe access for chimney repairs, repointing and stack removal. Usually a smaller, quicker job than full roof scaffolding.

🏚️ Roof Scaffolding

Full roof access for re-roofing, repairs and replacements, including flat roof work where access requirements differ from a pitched roof.

Find roof scaffolding near you →

🏗️ Extension Scaffolding

Scaffolding built around single and double storey extensions, coordinated with your builder's timeline as the structure goes up.

Find extension scaffolding near you →

🌧️ Gutter & Fascia Scaffolding

Lighter access scaffolding for gutter, fascia and soffit replacement — typically a shorter hire period than a full re-roof.

Find gutter scaffolding near you →

🎨 Render & Repointing Scaffolding

Full elevation access for external render, pebbledash and brick repointing work, where scaffolding needs to stay up for the whole facade.

Find render scaffolding near you →

🪜 Loft Conversion Scaffolding

Access for dormer and roof-light loft conversions, often combined with temporary weather protection while the roof is open.

Find loft conversion scaffolding near you →

☀️ Solar Panel Scaffolding

Roof access built specifically around solar panel installation, sized to the panel layout rather than a full re-roof.

Find solar panel scaffolding near you →

Commercial Scaffolding

Commercial jobs are usually larger, longer-running and need to work around a live business — an office, a retail unit or an industrial site can't always stop trading while access is being built.

Not sure which category fits your job? That's fine — most enquiries don't arrive pre-sorted into a category. Tell us what the work involves and we'll match it to the right type of access and give you one fixed price.

Why the Type of Scaffolding Affects Your Quote

The category of job matters because it changes what the scaffold actually needs to do: a chimney job needs a small, tall tower concentrated in one place, while a render job needs continuous access across an entire elevation. Getting this right upfront is part of why our contractors ask about the job before quoting — it's what makes a fixed price possible in the first place, rather than a rough estimate that changes once the scaffolders arrive on site.
