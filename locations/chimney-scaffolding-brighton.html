Chimney Scaffolding in Brighton — Local Area Guide

Brighton's Victorian and Regency housing stock is defined by its chimney stacks — rows of terrace houses across Hanover, Queens Park, Preston Park and Kemp Town all feature prominent chimney stacks that need regular maintenance given the city's coastal weather. Salt air, high winds off the English Channel and driving rain from the south all accelerate the deterioration of chimney mortar, flaunching and pots, making chimney scaffolding one of Brighton's most consistent scaffold requirements.

Chimney scaffolding in Brighton is most active on the hillside terraces of Hanover and the dense Victorian streets of Kemp Town and Queens Park. These properties' chimney stacks can be at significant height above the ridgeline, requiring scaffold access that a ladder simply cannot safely provide. Repointing, flaunching replacement and pot installation on Brighton chimneys needs proper platform access — particularly given the wind exposure on exposed hillside streets.
