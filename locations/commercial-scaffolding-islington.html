Commercial Scaffolding in Islington — Local Area Guide

Islington is an inner London borough immediately north of the City, known for its dense streets of Georgian and early Victorian terraces around squares such as Barnsbury and Canonbury, and the busy shopping and nightlife strip of Upper Street running up to Angel. It's one of London's most densely built boroughs, with very little open space beyond a scatter of garden squares and Highbury Fields, and property here commands some of the higher prices in north London despite the terraced, often narrow-fronted housing stock.

The borough's Georgian and Victorian terraces are typically four storeys including a lower ground floor, with steep slate roofs, parapets and party walls shared between neighbours — access for roofing or brickwork usually means scaffolding to the full height from a narrow street frontage. Many properties are listed or within conservation areas, adding planning considerations to any external work. Loft conversions are common given the premium on space, which also drives regular demand for scaffold access to rear additions and roof extensions.
